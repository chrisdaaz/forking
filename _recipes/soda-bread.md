title: "Soda Bread"
image: soda-bread.jpg

tags:
- bread
- desserts

ingredients:
- 4 cups flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1.5 sticks butter (unsalted)
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup raisins
- 1 tbs caraway seeds
- 1.5 cups buttermilk
- 1 egg yolk

directions:
- mix dry ingredients
- cut butter into mixture
- add raisins and caraway seeds
- gradually add buttermilk
- mix until you have a soft dough
- turn and knead on a lightly floured board for about one minute
- divide and shape into two loaves, place loaves on greased baking sheet
- flatten slightly and score top of each in a cross
- beat egg yolk with cold water and brush the top of each loaf
- Place in 350-degree oven and bake for about 1 hour, until done
